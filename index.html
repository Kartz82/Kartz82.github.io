STAGE 03 OF 05  ·  The warehouse explained what happened. Now: why are users not converting? GA4 event streams reveal funnel leakage, attribution shifts, and experiment outcomes.
Environment 03 // Conversion Command Center

Growth Funnel
Intelligence Engine

Raw GA4 event streams showed conversions, but not the complete user journey. I reconstructed 34,984 GA4 sessions into acquisition, funnel, attribution, and experimentation layers—revealing a 75.8% of landing-to-product drop-off, channel attribution shifts, and statistically insignificant experiment outcomes that prevented false optimization decisions.

// Conversion Step Analysis
1. Session Landing 34,984 · 100%
⚡ 75.8% lost here — critical break
2. Product View 8,467 · 24.2%
3. Cart Add 2,614 · 7.5%
4. Purchase 1,084 · 3.1%
Key Finding

75.8% of users dropped before reaching a product page. Direct traffic converted at significantly higher rates than organic traffic, while A/B testing showed no statistically significant uplift, preventing decisions based on noise.
